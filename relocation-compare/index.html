    Monthly payment illustration

    Same down payment, rate, and term on each metro’s typical home. Principal and interest is an illustration, not a quote. Property tax uses the metro effective rate. Insurance and mortgage insurance are assumptions you can change. They are labeled as assumptions below.

    Do not double-count district taxes. Metropolitan district, school, city, and county mill levies are property tax. They are already inside each metro’s effective tax rate, including Colorado Springs. The HOA / metro-district fee boxes are only for a separate monthly charge, such as HOA dues or a district operations fee that is not on the tax bill. Leave them at $0 unless you know there is one. Putting a mill levy here counts it twice.

    Formulas. Principal and interest is fully amortizing. Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12. Payment = loan × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate) raised to the minus number of months). A zero rate uses loan ÷ number of months. This is an illustration, not a quote.

    Property tax per year = home value × the metro effective rate. Per month = that ÷ 12. The rate is the Census median tax bill divided by the median home value, so city, school, and metropolitan-district mill levies are already in it.

    Homeowners insurance is an assumption. Per year = home value ÷ 1,000 × the dollars-per-thousand figure. Per month = that ÷ 12. The same figure is used for both metros. It is not a quote.

    Mortgage insurance is an assumption for a conventional loan, not FHA or VA. Per month = loan × the annual MI percent ÷ 12, and only when the down payment is under 20% (loan-to-value over 80%). Otherwise the line is $0. It is not a quote.

    Each line is rounded to the nearest dollar, then added, so the total matches the lines above.

    How each payment line is calculated

    Principal and interest uses a fully amortizing loan. Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12. Number of payments = term in years × 12. Payment = loan × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate) ^ −payments). If the rate is 0, payment = loan ÷ number of payments. The loan is the typical home value minus the down payment. This is an illustration, not a quote, a rate lock, or an offer to lend.

    Property tax, annual = typical home value × the metro effective rate. Monthly = annual ÷ 12. The rate is the Census median real-estate tax divided by the median home value, not a levy on one house.

    Homeowners insurance is an adjustable assumption, not a quote and not a figure from the data file. Annual premium = home value ÷ 1,000 × the dollars-per-thousand input. Monthly = annual ÷ 12. The same dollars-per-thousand is applied to both metros, so the dollar gap follows home value. A real premium depends on the carrier, deductible, roof, and local risk. Hail along the Front Range, and wind and hail in other states, often price differently. Replace the assumption with a quote before you rely on it.

    Mortgage insurance is an adjustable assumption for a conventional illustration, not an insurer’s rate sheet and not FHA or VA. It is included only when the down payment is under 20%, which is loan-to-value over 80%. Monthly MI = loan × the annual MI percent ÷ 12. At 20% down or more, the line is $0. Real mortgage insurance depends on credit, loan-to-value, and the insurer.

    The total adds the rounded dollar lines. Each line is rounded to the nearest dollar, then added, so the total matches the lines you see.

    Drop the down payment below 20% if you want to see the mortgage-insurance line turn on. At the starting 20% it is $0, which is what this conventional illustration should show.

    About the data

    • Typical home value and trend: Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI), all homes, middle third, smoothed and seasonally adjusted, by metro, through 2026-09-30 (zillow.com/research/data, downloaded Nov 4, 2026). The chart uses quarterly points from 2021-09 to 2026-09.
    • Effective property tax rate: median real estate taxes paid divided by median home value for owner-occupied homes in the metro, U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2020-2024 5-year estimates (tables B25103 and B25077, via Census Reporter). A metro-wide average; individual bills vary by county, district and assessed value.
    • Cost of living: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parities, all items, 2024 (released Feb 2026). U.S. average = 100. BEA publishes this only for metropolitan areas, so smaller micropolitan areas show “not available”.
    • Zillow metro names are matched to Census and BEA metros by principal city and state.
    • Not in those files: the interest rate, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, and HOA or metro-district fee. Those are the assumptions in the payment section, with the formulas next to them. Insurance and mortgage insurance are adjustable assumptions, not quotes. The optional fee is not property tax.
    • The files are not newer than the dates above. Nothing on this page is a live Zillow feed, a current tax bill, or a rate quote.
    • Sample PDF, built from this page’s default illustration (20% down, 7.00% rate, 30 years, $5.00 insurance per $1,000 of value per year, 0.50% mortgage insurance only if loan-to-value is over 80%, HOA $0): austin-vs-colorado-springs.pdf.
