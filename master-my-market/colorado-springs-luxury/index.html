Colorado Springs at the top of the market

A neighborhood-by-neighborhood look at homes from $750,000 and up across north Colorado Springs, Monument, Black Forest and the Broadmoor area. Prices, 5-year trends, schools, property taxes and the lifestyle each area offers.

Military & employers

Five installations anchor the economy: Fort Carson, Peterson and Schriever Space Force Bases, Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station and the U.S. Air Force Academy. Military makes up over 24% of local jobs. Large private employers include Amazon, The Broadmoor, Compassion International, UCHealth and Penrose-St. Francis.

The outdoors

Garden of the Gods, Pikes Peak, Cheyenne Cañon, the Air Force Academy trails and the U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Training Center are all within the metro, with ski areas a few hours west.

Property taxes

Colorado's residential property taxes are among the lowest in the country. In these upscale areas, expect roughly 0.4% to 0.6% of value a year, before any metro district levy. Estimates for each area are below.
