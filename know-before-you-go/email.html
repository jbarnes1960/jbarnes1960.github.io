Selling and moving?

Know Before You Go

Selling your home is the easy part. The surprise often comes when it's time to buy the next one. Some sellers list, get an offer, and only then learn they can't get the new mortgage they counted on, or that their sale nets less than they planned.

Check first, so you can plan:

Credit issues
A late payment or high card balance can change your loan options and cost, and may need time to fix.
Income changes
A new job, self-employment, or retirement is counted differently by lenders.
Higher costs in the new area
Taxes, insurance, and HOA dues all count toward your qualifying payment.
Unexpected liens
A judgment, tax, HOA, or contractor's lien can surface at title and delay or stop your sale. Ask your title company for a preliminary title search so there's time to clear it.
A higher payoff than you expect
Daily interest, fees, an escrow shortage, or a prepayment charge can make your mortgage, HELOC, or second-loan payoff higher than your statement. Request a payoff statement early so your sale proceeds and down payment are real.
Problems you don't know about yet
Consider a pre-listing home inspection. Hail damage to the roof, foundation movement, or anything needing an insurance claim and adjuster visit takes time to schedule and fix. Better to find it before you're on the MLS than after a buyer's inspection.
