Selling and moving?

Know Before You Go

Selling your home is the easy part. The surprise often comes when it's time to buy the next one. Some sellers list, get an offer, and only then learn they can't get the new mortgage they counted on.

Check first, so you can plan:

  • Credit issues can change your loan options and cost, and may need time to fix.
  • Income changes (new job, self-employed, retiring) are counted differently by lenders.
  • Higher costs in the new area (taxes, insurance, HOA) all count toward your qualifying payment.

Inside the free guide:

  • A quick check that shows your debt-to-income ratios and the loan programs you'd likely fit
  • A "where you are vs. where you're moving" cost comparison
  • A lender document checklist and a moving checklist
