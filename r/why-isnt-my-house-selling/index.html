What your diagnosis includes

The same sections as a full listing diagnosis — prepared by hand, in plain English.

  • The short answer

    A clear verdict on what’s holding the home back, plus a confidence level and the primary reason — so you know where to start.

  • Your home vs the market

    List price vs the area’s median sold price, price per square foot, days on market, and any price reductions against the original list price.

  • This home vs the comps

    Recent nearby closings, shown against your listing. Differences in beds, baths, square feet, price, price per square foot and distance are noted under each figure.

  • Local conditions

    Months of supply (buyer’s or seller’s market), sale-to-list ratio, median days on market, active listings, and the year-over-year price change in the submarket.

  • Twelve-month price trend

    Median sale price per month for this submarket, as reported. Months with no reported data are left out.

  • What’s working against it

    The specific issues weighing on the listing, each with a severity level, a short explanation, and the evidence behind it.

  • What to do about it

    A practical, numbered action plan — the next steps that actually move a sitting listing, in order.
