How the comps were chosen. I started with the 210 closed single-family sales in the last 12 months within 2 miles of the subject (Redfin map search run 10/28/2026): 50 are within 0.5 mi and 76 within 1 mi. Order of preference: same subdivision, then within 0.5 mi, then within 1 mi; closed within 6 months (90 days preferred); GLA within about ±15% of 3,440 sq ft; similar age (within about 10 years), no private pool, similar bedrooms and stories; bracketing the subject on GLA rather than on price. Result: all 5 comps are in the subject's own community (Oaks at San Gabriel), 0.05-0.20 mi away; none is beyond 1 mile, so no distance justification was needed. Closed dates run from 6/4/2026 to 9/16/2026; three are within 90 days. Comps 4 and 5 are older than 90 days (146 and 132 days) but inside 6 months. They are kept because they are the only closed same-community sales I found within 6 months that are meaningfully larger than the subject (+12.3% and +11.1% GLA), so they bracket the subject from above; comps 1 and 3 bracket it from below. Their extra bedroom, bath and second story are not priced (see rates). Pending and active listings were not used as comps. Interiors and condition were not inspected. Sources: Redfin listing pages (linked) for GLA, beds/baths, year built, lot, garage, pool and tax records; subject details from the Keenan Group listing and realty.com.

Adjustment rates and their basis
Time (market conditions)−0.34% per monthZIP 78628 median $/sq ft is −4.1% year over year (OfferBacked, 3 months to 8/31/2026), divided by 12 and applied from each comp's close date to 10/28/2026. It is a ZIP-wide trend, so it is weak evidence for one community; the effect is only 0.5-1.6% of price.
Gross living area$189/sq ftLeast-squares slope of last list price on GLA across 8 closed resales in the same community within 0.5 mi in the last 12 months with no private pool (comps 1-5 plus 1813 Cherry Glade, 234 Rio Ranchero, 105 Bull Ridge): slope $189/sq ft, R² = 0.65, n = 8. Prices in that regression are last list prices (an agent-supplied actual sold price replaces the list price where one was given) and are not time-adjusted. Applied to the full GLA difference (it also absorbs the extra bedroom, bath and second-floor space in the larger homes, which avoids double counting).
Location$0All five comps are in Oaks at San Gabriel (same HOA) within 0.20 mi; HOA dues are similar. No paired-sale evidence of a difference between sections.
Site / lot$0County land value is $104,000 for every comp (2026 roll, Redfin tax history) and for the subject (realty.com tax record), so the appraisal district sees no value difference. Lot sizes differ (7,749-17,755 sq ft vs 8,986); no paired sales support a per-sq-ft lot rate.
Age$0Comps are built 2014-2020 vs 2015 (within 5 years). The data has no paired sales isolating an age effect, and GLA already explains most of the price variation.
Bedrooms / bathrooms / stories$0Not adjusted: no paired-sale evidence in this data set, and the GLA rate already carries the space difference. Differences are displayed in the grid. Garage$0No market evidence for a rate. Subject has 2 spaces plus a flex space for a workshop or golf cart (per listing); comps have 2-3 spaces.
