What's my home worth?

Your net sheet is only as good as your sales price. Here's how a real home value gets figured out.

How James picks comparable sales ("comps")

  1. Close to you. Same subdivision first, then within half a mile, then within one mile.
  2. Recently closed. Homes that actually sold in the last 6 months (the last 90 days is best). Homes still for sale are only context.
  3. Similar homes. Square footage within about 15%, similar age, style, beds and baths, lot, garage, pool and condition.
  4. At least 3 closed comps, ideally 3 to 6, that bracket your home.
  5. Adjustments. Each comp is adjusted for differences (size, age, beds, baths, garage, pool, condition, and time since it sold), and the adjusted value is rounded to the nearest $1,000.

Comparative Market Analysis prepared by a loan officer using appraisal-style comp selection and adjustments. This is not an appraisal and was not prepared under USPAP by a licensed appraiser.
