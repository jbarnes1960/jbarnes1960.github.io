DOWN PAYMENT HELP IN TEXAS
TDHCA · State program
My First Texas Home
From the Texas Dept. of Housing & Community Affairs. For first-time buyers (or anyone who hasn't owned in 3 years, and veterans): a 30-year fixed loan with optional down payment and closing cost help.
Income and purchase-price limits apply. tdhca.texas.gov
TSAHC · Heroes
Homes for Texas Heroes
From the Texas State Affordable Housing Corp. For teachers, police, firefighters, EMS, corrections officers, veterans and other eligible public servants: down payment help with a 30-year fixed loan.
Income limits apply. tsahc.org
TSAHC · Moderate income
Home Sweet Texas
For low- and moderate-income buyers who aren't in a hero profession; first-time status not required. Down payment help with a 30-year fixed loan.
Income limits apply. tsahc.org
