When to use each

Aven: the only fully fixed-rate option. Smallest and largest lines ($5K to $1M) and the highest DTI (55%). Needs 620 FICO; not available in NY, NV, MA, or HI.

NEXA Figure: fastest, closing in about 5 days. Most flexible on property and vesting: high-rise condos, third liens, and LLCs on non-primary homes. One applicant only; 600–639 FICO is limited to a second lien up to $125K.

NEXA HELIX: variable rate, interest-only during the draw. Most lenient on property type (non-warrantable condos, modular). Highest fees; Texas primary homes always need a full appraisal.

NEXA NFTYDoor: variable rate, interest-only during the draw. Up to 90% CLTV at 720+ for lines up to $500K, lowest fee (up to 2.39%), and easiest for second homes and investment properties (640 FICO).

Texas & Colorado

Colorado: none of the four programs exclude it, and no Colorado-specific rules are listed.

Texas: every program adds limits, mainly an 80% CLTV cap, primary residence only, and capped fees.
