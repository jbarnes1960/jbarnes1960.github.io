FOUR WAYS TO BUY FIRST
Option 1 • Both homes
Buy first, list later
If your income can comfortably carry both payments, you may qualify with both homes on the books and use savings for the down payment — no bridge loan needed.
✓ Upside: One move, on your timeline.
⚠ Keep in mind: You carry both housing payments until your old home sells. Terry can help plan the timing and the sale.
Talk timing with Terry · (719) 491-8966 →Text James about financing →
Option 2 • Equity line
HELOC on your current home
Open a home equity line of credit before you list, draw what you need for the down payment, then pay it off when your current home sells. Nexa HELOCs range from $5K to $1M, with FICO scores as low as 600.
✓ Upside: Flexible — you only draw what you need.
⚠ Keep in mind: It must be in place before the home goes on the market. Most HELOCs have a variable rate, your home secures the line, and a lender can freeze it if your home’s value drops (per the CFPB).
Call Terry · (719) 491-8966 →Text James about a HELOC →
Option 3 • Loan
Bridge loan
A short-term loan against your current home that covers the down payment on the new one. It typically runs about 6–12 months and is paid off when your old home sells.
✓ Upside: Make a strong offer with no home-sale contingency.
⚠ Keep in mind: Rates run higher, and you carry two payments for a short time. Plan how it will be repaid.
Call Terry · (719) 491-8966 →Text James about a bridge loan →
Option 4 • After the sale
Recast after you sell
When your old home sells, you can put the proceeds toward your new loan and ask your lender about a recalculated payment.
✓ Upside: May lower your monthly payment after the sale.
⚠ Keep in mind: Availability and fees depend on your loan type and lender.
