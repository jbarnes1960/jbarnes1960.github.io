622 Campus St · Home-Buying Steps

Your Path to Closing

Prequal → offer → inspection / appraisal → closing

1

Get pre-approved

Talk with James and complete the application so he can review income, assets and credit. A pre-approval letter shows sellers you are serious and clarifies your real price range before you tour.

2

Tour the home and make an offer

Zach Backor of Keeping It Realty can arrange a showing of 622 Campus Street. When you are ready, Zach helps you write an offer: price, earnest money, financing type, inspection and closing dates. Include your pre-approval letter.

3

Inspection and appraisal

Once under contract, schedule the home inspection and negotiate repairs within contract deadlines. Your lender orders the appraisal while underwriting verifies your file and issues clear-to-close.

4

Closing

You receive your Closing Disclosure at least three business days before closing. Do a final walk-through, bring cash to close as instructed by the title company, sign, and get your keys.

Typical timeline
Timing depends on the contract dates, your documents, and appraisal and title turn times. Responding quickly to document requests is the best way to keep the closing date.

622 Campus St · Pre-Approval Checklist

Pre-Approval Document Checklist

Having these ready speeds approval. Your exact list depends on program and situation.

  • Government-issued photo ID and Social Security number
  • Pay stubs for the most recent 30 days
  • W-2s for the last 2 years
  • Federal tax returns for the last 2 years (all pages and schedules)
  • Bank and savings statements for the last 2 months (all pages)
  • Retirement / investment statements if used for down payment or reserves
  • Gift letter and donor bank statement, if any funds are a gift
  • Self-employed / 1099: 2 years of returns, YTD P&L, and business bank statements if requested
  • Landlord contact or 12 months of rent payments, and 2-year address history
  • VA buyers: Certificate of Eligibility (James can request it) and DD-214 or statement of service
  • Divorce decree or support documents, if applicable
  • Letters of explanation for inquiries, employment gaps or large deposits, if requested
Texas down payment assistance: TSAHC & TDHCA

Texas offers several down-payment / closing-cost programs. TSAHC describes assistance of about 2-5% of the loan via grant or deferred second-lien options under programs including Home Sweet Texas and Homes for Texas Heroes, plus Mortgage Credit Certificates for eligible buyers. TDHCA My First / My Choice Texas Home programs similarly offer 2-5% assistance. Income and purchase-price limits apply; minimum FICO often 620. Sources: tsahc.org/homebuyers/homebuyer-programs and James’s Texas DPA summary (mtg.broker, Nov 1, 2026). Ask James whether a TSAHC or TDHCA option could fit; this is not a commitment to lend.
