1Who qualifies

  • Non-U.S. residents and non-permanent visa holders with income or assets abroad
  • Buying a second home or investment property (not a primary residence)
  • No U.S. SSN or credit score required on many programs; some ask for a credit reference letter or U.S. tradelines if you have them
  • Citizens of OFAC-sanctioned countries are not eligible

2Typical terms

  • Purchase: up to 75% LTV; 70% is common
  • Cash-out refi: typically 60–65% (up to 70% on some)
  • LTV steps down as loan size rises (roughly 55–65% near $3M)
  • Reserves: typically 6–12 months PITIA (lower on some smaller loans)
  • Interest-only and 30-year fixed or ARM options on many programs

3Documents to expect

  • Valid passport + unexpired visa where applicable (Canadian citizens don’t need a visa; some programs accept the visa waiver program)
  • International credit report or 1–3 credit/bank reference letters
  • 2 months of bank or asset statements, sourced and seasoned
  • Lease or appraiser market rent for DSCR; CPA letter for full-doc income
  • Funds generally need to be in a U.S. account or wired to title before closing

4Property & qualifying

  • DSCR option: qualify on the rent ÷ PITIA — typically 1.00 or better; some lower-ratio options at reduced LTV
  • Asset-qualifier and full-doc options on select programs
  • 1–4 unit homes, townhomes, PUDs, warrantable condos; non-warrantable condos and condotels on select programs
  • Short-term rentals considered (typically with an LTV cut)
  • Individual or LLC vesting on select programs

5Closing from abroad

Limited power of attorney is available on select programs, and documents signed overseas are generally notarized by a U.S. embassy or consular official. I’ll confirm what your lender requires before you travel.
