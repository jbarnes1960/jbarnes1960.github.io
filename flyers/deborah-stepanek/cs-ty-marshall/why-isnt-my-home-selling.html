What I'd do next
Recommendations to discuss with the seller and listing agent. Pricing decisions belong to them.
1
Price into the last-list band of the same plan
A move into the mid-$480s to low-$490s (about $190–$195/sf) would sit near 4322 Erika Ct's last list and just above the $474K same-plan closed last lists, while staying above the pending larger Corbin Ct at $461,420. These are discussion points from public last lists, not an appraisal.
2
Call out the upgrades vs builder inventory
Lead photos and remarks with what a new D.R. Horton at $496K may not have: custom blinds, upgraded hardware, gutters, backyard lighting, landscaping. Make the value gap obvious.
3
Refresh marketing after any price change
Broker open, public open, price-change blast to agents and saved-search buyers, and a headline that names the new price.
4
Offer a buyer credit once a rate is set
James can model a 2–3% seller credit vs a price cut once a current rate is available — no payment figures are in this report.
