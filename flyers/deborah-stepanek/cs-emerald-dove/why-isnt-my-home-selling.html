What I'd do next
Recommendations to discuss with the seller and listing agent.
1
Clear the MLS status first
Confirm whether the home is Active, Pending, or Temporarily Off Market. Portal conflicts confuse buyers and agents.
2
Price into the closed last-list $/sf band
A discussion range near $162–$170/sf on 1,923 sf is roughly $311K–$327K, or a softer step toward the mid-$330s to sit near 803 Emerald Dove's $337,500 ask. These are last-list discussion points, not an appraisal.
3
Refresh photos and staging notes
If interiors are virtually staged, say so clearly; add a floor plan and emphasize the corner lot and 4 bedrooms.
