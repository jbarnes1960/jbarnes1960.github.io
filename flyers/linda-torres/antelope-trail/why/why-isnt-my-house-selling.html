Findings at a glance
Still early in this listing run. Listed Oct 22, 2026 at $319,000 (MLS #628415) with no price cuts found. About 14–15 days on market as of Nov 5, 2026 — too soon to treat “not selling” as a failure; this check-up is about positioning.
Ask sits above Temple’s median list and above nearby new-build asks. Temple median list was about $280,000 in the Realty.com market panel (Oct 2026). Nearby 2026 new builds on Campbelton Dr advertise roughly $261k–$287k for 4/2 homes ~1,859–1,922 sf (ezhomesearch nearby list) — different product, but they set a lower entry point buyers will see.
Tax assessment is above the ask (unusual). Assessor feed on Realty.com shows 2025 assessed total $384,580 with tax billed ~$6,137; BHHS/ezhomesearch show ~$6,490 tax. List price $319,000 is below that assessed figure — so the friction is less “tax says it’s cheaper” and more “buyers comparing to newer/cheaper inventory.”
What is working on paper. 2022 build, open family room, covered patio, custom exterior lighting, ~25 minutes to Fort Cavazos per remarks, and financing terms include Conventional, FHA and VA.
