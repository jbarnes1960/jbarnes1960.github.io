Hi [Partner Name],

Here's your November snapshot for Colorado Springs & Pikes Peak.

Rates: Colorado conventional pricing moved during Friday, Nov. 6, and ended at the same level as Thursday afternoon. That kind of movement is why I confirm each buyer's numbers in real time.

Market context: Mortgage News Daily reported Nov 6 that average lender rates held at their lowest level since September 22, even after a stronger-than-expected jobs report. Freddie Mac's weekly survey (Nov 5) showed a third straight weekly decline. MBS Highway's National Housing Index fell to 15 in October, its fifth straight monthly drop, signaling softer national buyer activity.

Inventory (Pikes Peak region, El Paso and Teller counties): October 2026 figures based on Pikes Peak REALTOR data show 4,110 active listings, up 5% from a year earlier, or about 5.0 months of supply. Homes averaged 57 days to go under contract, versus 54 last October. Buyers have more choice, and well-prepared offers still stand out.

Winning tight offers together: When it fits the buyer, we can work toward a fully underwritten approval before they write.
