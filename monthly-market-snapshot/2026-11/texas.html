Central Texas: Rates, Inventory & Winning Tight Offers

Hi [Partner Name],

Here's your November snapshot for Central Texas.

Rates: Lender pricing has been moving quickly, sometimes within a single day, so I'm confirming each buyer's numbers in real time rather than relying on last week's quote.

Market context: Mortgage News Daily reported Nov 6 that average lender rates held at their lowest level since September 22, even after a stronger-than-expected jobs report. Freddie Mac's weekly survey (Nov 5) showed a third straight weekly decline. MBS Highway's National Housing Index fell to 15 in October, its fifth straight monthly drop, signaling softer national buyer activity.

Inventory (Austin metro as our Central Texas benchmark): Unlock MLS's latest report (September 2026, published Oct 13) shows 13,465 active listings in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro, down 3.4% from a year earlier, with 5.4 months of inventory. Homes closed at an average 92.7% of list price, so pricing and terms are still negotiable. This metro data is not a statewide or Temple-Killeen-Belton figure.

Winning tight offers together: When it fits the buyer, we can work toward a fully underwritten approval before they write. I'll call the listing agent to learn what the seller values most and confirm a closing timeline I can verify. We'll compete on clarity and responsiveness, never by waiving buyer protections.
