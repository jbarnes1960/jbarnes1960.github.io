Protect your approval through closing
Now That You Have Applied for Your Home Loan —
What NOT to Do!
A simple guide to protecting your mortgage approval from application through closing.
View the flyer → Download the PDF
 
IMPORTANT: Your lender may recheck your credit, employment, assets, and debts before closing. Even small changes can delay your closing or affect loan approval.
1
DO NOT open new credit accounts
Avoid new credit cards, store cards, furniture financing, buy-now-pay-later plans, auto loans, and co-signing. Even a “no payments for 12 months” offer can create a new debt.
2
DO NOT make large purchases on credit
Wait until after closing to buy appliances, furniture, vehicles, or other expensive items. New monthly payments can change your debt-to-income ratio.
3
DO NOT quit, switch jobs, or change how you are paid without talking to us
A new employer, reduced hours, moving from salary to commission, or becoming self-employed may require new documentation or change qualifying income.
4
DO NOT miss or pay bills late
Continue making every mortgage, rent, credit card, auto, student loan, and other payment on time—even accounts you expect to pay off at closing.
5
DO NOT move money around without a paper trail
Avoid unexplained large deposits, cash deposits, transfers between accounts, or moving funds into new accounts. Ask us how to document transfers, gifts, and sale proceeds.
6
DO NOT spend your down payment or closing funds
Keep the money needed for closing, required reserves, and your earnest money available and verifiable. Do not assume an expected refund or bonus will arrive in time.
7
DO NOT pay off or close debts without checking first
Paying off a loan, closing a card, or disputing a credit item can affect your credit profile or available cash. Let us advise on the timing and documentation.
8
DO NOT ignore requests from your lender
Respond quickly to requests for pay stubs, bank statements, explanations, insurance information, and signatures. Delays can jeopardize contract deadlines.
9
DO NOT change your bank accounts or payroll deposits unexpectedly
Changing banks, direct deposit, or account ownership may require fresh statements and additional verification.
10
DO NOT make major changes to your purchase or finances
Before changing the sales contract, taking on new obligations, receiving a large gift, selling investments, or making major withdrawals, contact your loan officer.
11
DO NOT assume you are fully approved until the loan closes
A preapproval, conditional approval, or “clear to close” is not the same as a funded loan. Keep your financial situation stable until closing and funding.
12
DO NOT wire closing funds using unverified instructions
Real estate wire fraud is common. Independently call your title or closing company using a trusted phone number before sending any wire; never rely on last-minute emailed changes.
WHEN IN DOUBT, ASK FIRST
Before making any financial, employment, or credit change, call your loan officer. A quick conversation today may prevent a costly delay tomorrow.
