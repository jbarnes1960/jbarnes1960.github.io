DSCR loans (non-QM investor option)

Not an agency guideline. A DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loan is a non-QM investor product. Each lender or investor sets its own rules, so every number here is lender-specific. I could not find a public Nexa Lending or UWM matrix for it, so whether Nexa/UWM offers DSCR, and on what terms, is Verify with underwriting.
ItemDSCR loan (typical structure)
How it qualifiesQualifies on the property's cash flow, usually monthly rent ÷ PITIA, instead of personal income and DTI. A common shorthand is "DSCR of 1.0 or higher," but the minimum ratio is set by the lender: Verify with underwriting
Who it fitsInvestors being qualified on the property's cash flow rather than personal income. Availability and eligibility: Verify with underwriting
Down payment / max LTVLender-specific. Exact LTV by unit count, credit score and purchase vs cash-out: Verify with underwriting
Credit score, reserves, prepayment penaltyLender-specific: Verify with underwriting. Prepayment penalty structure: Verify with underwriting
