Before writing an offer near $220,000

  • See the inside first. Renovated homes nearby closed at last list prices of $207,000–$255,000; dated or as-is homes at $119,999–$178,999. If this home is not updated, $220,000 is above every as-is comp.
  • Plan for the appraisal. The lender's appraisal must support the price. If it comes in lower, the buyer pays the gap, renegotiates or walks away under the contract terms.
  • FHA and VA appraisals also check the home's condition (safety, soundness and security). A dated home may need repairs before closing.
  • Property taxes: this home's tax bill was not verified. Check the Dallas CAD record; a buyer's homestead exemption can lower the bill.
  • Get pre-approved so the buyer knows the program, cash needed and payment before touring.
