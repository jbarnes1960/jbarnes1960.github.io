Weighting. All five comps are the same plan with $0 net adjustments, so weights are equal (20% each) and the indicated value is the simple average of the five last list prices: $184,000 (median $190,000; range $169,990 to $189,990; middle half $180,000 to $190,000). Dropping any one comp moves the average to between $182,000 and $187,000. The three Campus St sales alone average $190,000. Average $/sq ft of the five is $157, which × 1,174 sq ft is about $184,000. Result. Suggested value about $184,000 (about $157 per sq ft), with a suggested range of $170,000 to $190,000 (full last-list spread $169,990–$189,990). Vs list price: the active ask of $179,400 sits $4,600 below the indicated average and about $10,590 below the Campus St cluster average — consistent with a resale asking under recent builder last-list levels. Reasons for caution: Texas prices are last lists, not closed prices; several builder sales advertised rate/closing incentives; the subject is a resale; and 614/618 close dates come from aggregator sold feeds (Movoto/Trulia) cross-checked to last-list on listing pages. A licensed appraiser or your MLS with real closed prices should confirm.
