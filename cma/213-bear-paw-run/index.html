Weighting. Each comp is weighted by the inverse of its gross adjustment % (fewest adjustments = most weight): comp 1 (118 Bear Paw Run, 8.7% gross) carries 39%, and the others share the rest. Sensitivity. The GLA rate drives the result: at $122 minus or plus $25 per sq ft the indicated value moves to $480,000 or $484,000. Texas sale prices are not public; if these homes closed at the 97.7% ZIP median sale-to-list ratio, the indicated value would be about $471,000 (sensitivity only, not applied). The subject's last list of $624,000 is above the indicated value. Story, garage, builder/age and condition differences could not be priced from market data and were left unadjusted. A licensed appraiser with closed prices, interiors and condition would refine this.
